04.About

Willemijn Post is a senior project manager with over ten years of experience in advertising, media and content, running large projects with big budgets and leading large teams.

She started out at agencies such as Fresh Communications and CCCP, grew into project management at Nobla and Social1nfluencers, and went on to Talpa Network, XITE and dentsuACHTUNG!. Today she works as a senior project manager at ACE in Amsterdam. She combines a sharp eye for detail with a strong sense of overview, and is known for keeping complex projects calm, organised and on track. Outside of advertising she has renovated several properties in the Netherlands from start to finish, bringing the same hands-on project discipline to design, contractors and budgets.
